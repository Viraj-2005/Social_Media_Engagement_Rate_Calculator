Engagement Formulas
Video-Level Metrics
Total Engagement = Likes + Comments
Engagement Rate = (Total Engagement / Views) × 100
Like Rate = (Likes / Views) × 100
Comment Rate = (Comments / Views) × 100
Channel-Level Metrics
Avg Video ER = Σ(Video ER) / Video Count
Overall Channel ER = (Total Likes + Total Comments) / Total Views × 100

These are distinct metrics. Average ER measures consistency; Overall ER measures total audience interaction.
